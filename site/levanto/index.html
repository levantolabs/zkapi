Use Sage with zkAPI. Deposit ETH once, and every call runs on a temporary key that can't be linked to your wallet.

  1. Deposit ETH once.
  2. Get a key for your agent. It lasts up to 30 days.
  3. Your agent calls Sage. You pay only what it uses.

Deposits and withdrawals are public on Ethereum; your agent's calls can't be linked to them. Experimental: keep deposits small, and withdraw before clearing this browser's data. zkAPI on GitHub ↗
